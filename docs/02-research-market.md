# YourPower — Market and Competitor Research

*Research completed Oct 8, 2026.*

## The open lane

Nobody owns "Duolingo-for-EE": a game with real simulation fidelity, a genuine curriculum arc through Circuit Analysis, and modern retention mechanics. Circuit Jam proves demand for circuit puzzles; its ceiling is this game's floor.

## Competitors

### EveryCircuit (MuseMaze) — the mobile sim gold standard
- Animated current flow, live parameter tweaking, mini-oscilloscopes on wires, AC/Bode analysis. The interaction model to beat.
- **Gaps:** pure sandbox — no progression, goals, or curriculum. Community library uncurated. Noted Android stability issues.
- Freemium, ~$14.99 full unlock.

### Circuit Jam (same creators as EveryCircuit) — the closest thing to a game
- 100+ puzzles, now free without ads. Unlocks components as sandbox parts. Teaches Ohm's law, KCL/KVL, equivalent R/C.
- **Gaps:** caps out at intro electronics — nothing on nodal/mesh analysis, phasors, op-amps, or frequency domain. Linear, aging puzzle design. No social/competitive layer.
- **Lesson:** validate the campaign→sandbox-unlock structure, but go far deeper on curriculum.

### iCircuit — the power-user tool
- CAD-like schematic entry, analog + digital, always-on analysis, multimeter/oscilloscope. Paid, proprietary.
- **Gaps:** zero teaching layer, dated UX. Not a competitor for learners — but proof that serious sim on mobile sells.

### Electronics Puzzle Lab 1 & 2 (consoles/PC, $4.99)
- Flip-switches-and-light-LEDs puzzles.
- **Key warning from reviewers:** it *assumes* prior knowledge rather than teaching — without a teaching layer, puzzles become guesswork. YourPower must teach each concept *before* testing it.

### Brilliant / PhET / Tinkercad Circuits
- Brilliant: polished interactive lessons but shallow on EE (math/physics/CS skew), no real simulator.
- PhET and Tinkercad: great classroom sandboxes, browser-only, no gamification.

## Positioning

| | EveryCircuit | Circuit Jam | YourPower |
|---|---|---|---|
| Real simulation | ✅ | Simplified | ✅ (MNA core) |
| Curriculum arc | ❌ | Intro only | ✅ Full Circuit Analysis I+ |
| Game progression | ❌ | Basic | ✅ XP, leagues, optimization scoring |
| Teaching layer | ❌ | Thin | ✅ Learn-by-doing per concept |
